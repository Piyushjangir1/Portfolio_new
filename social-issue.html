Social Issue — Water Scarcity & Groundwater Depletion (Rajasthan)

Rajasthan faces recurring water stress due to low rainfall, over-extraction of groundwater, and inefficient water use in agriculture. This reduces drinking water availability, impacts agriculture-based livelihoods, and increases migration pressure.

Why it matters

  • Many rural communities depend on groundwater for drinking and crops.
  • Overuse of groundwater leads to falling water tables and saline intrusion.
  • Water scarcity increases vulnerability during droughts and affects livelihoods.

Practical Solutions

  1. Rainwater harvesting at household and community level.
  2. Micro-irrigation (drip/sprinkler) to reduce farm water use.
  3. Fixed water budgets and monitored groundwater extraction.
  4. Awareness programs for water-efficient crops & practices.
  5. Local recharge projects (check dams, percolation tanks).
